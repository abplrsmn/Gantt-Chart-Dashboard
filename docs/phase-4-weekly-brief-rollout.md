# Phase 4 — Weekly Brief & Rollout

## Goal
Turn the framework into a repeatable operational routine so the system is used weekly, not just observed occasionally.

## Duration
Week 7–8

## Main Focus
- create weekly brief structure
- define recurring review rhythm
- align role summaries with operational needs
- prepare the dashboard and summary process for regular use

## Scope
### 1. Weekly Brief Structure
The weekly brief should summarize:
- total active projects
- projects on pace
- projects delayed
- projects near deadline
- projects in handover
- top blockers
- actions for next week

### 2. Role-Based Weekly Follow-Up
Each role should know:
- what changed this week
- what is blocked
- what needs action next week
- what requires escalation

### 3. Management Snapshot
Management summary should be concise and focused on:
- risk concentration
- major delays
- important handovers
- priority follow-up items

### 4. Operational Rhythm
Recommended cadence:
- daily summary for quick visibility
- weekly brief for structured review
- role-based follow-up after brief review

## Deliverables
- weekly brief template
- basic role-based weekly summary flow
- operational review rhythm
- rollout-ready project monitoring routine

## Success Check
At the end of this phase, the organization should be able to:
- review weekly progress quickly
- identify delays by stage
- know what each role needs to do next
- use the dashboard as an operational control tool
